# DPN PlantPulse Product Vision

## Mission

Turn a plant photograph into an ongoing intelligence record instead of a one-time answer.

## Core loop

**SEE → UNDERSTAND → ACT → REMEMBER → PREDICT**

### See
Capture a live photo or select an existing image.

### Understand
Identify likely species and surface confidence-aware visual health signals.

### Act
Prioritize care actions rather than dumping generic plant-care text.

### Remember
Store scans, care events, treatments, environmental changes, and outcomes.

### Predict
Use repeated observations to detect health decline and estimate what intervention deserves attention next.

## Health score principle

The PlantPulse Score must be explainable. A score should be decomposable into signals such as visible leaf health, hydration indicators, disease risk, pest risk, nutrient stress, light context, environmental telemetry, and history. A production model should never present a number without making the contributing evidence available.

## Safety

Plant identification from images is uncertain. PlantPulse must preserve confidence language, especially around toxicity, edibility, treatment, pesticides, pets, children and livestock. High-impact guidance should require stronger evidence and authoritative knowledge sources.
